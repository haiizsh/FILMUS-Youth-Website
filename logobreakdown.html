Logo Elements Breakdown

Brand identity

Every detail of the FILMUS Youth logo reflects our commitment to empowerment, unity, and growth.

🩷

Pink Color

The pink color represents femininity, compassion, modesty, and empowerment. It symbolizes the strength of women who lead with both courage and kindness. Pink reflects care, solidarity, and nurturing leadership — values central to FILMUS Youth's mission. It reminds us that women's leadership can be both strong and compassionate, promoting peace, understanding, and unity.

👩‍👩‍👧‍👦

Diverse Women Figures

The logo features women with different appearances, clothing, and hairstyles, representing diversity and inclusivity. This symbolizes that women from different religions, ethnicities, cultures, and backgrounds are welcomed and valued in FILMUS Youth. It reflects our commitment to creating a safe and inclusive space where Muslim and non‑Muslim women can grow, collaborate, and lead together.

👭

Collective Woman Shape

All the women in the logo come together to form one large female figure. This symbolizes unity among women — that despite differences in beliefs, culture, or background, women are stronger when they stand together. It represents the idea that every woman contributes to a larger movement of empowerment and change.

🌱

Floral & Nature Elements

The flowers and organic shapes surrounding the women symbolize growth, resilience, and transformation. Just like flowers grow and bloom, FILMUS Youth aims to help women develop their potential, confidence, and leadership abilities. These elements also represent hope, renewal, and positive change within communities.
